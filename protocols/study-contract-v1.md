# Study, gym and portfolio contract v1

`nf_core::study::{StudyContract,GymPortfolio,GymManifest}` are strict versioned Rust records. `Validated::parse` enforces bounded canonical JSON, unknown-field/enum/version rejection and all nested semantic checks. `StudyContract::digest` validates and hashes the complete typed canonical record. The records do not run a study, prove a receipt, measure a score, grant filesystem/network access, or accept a model.

## Identity and authority

The contract binds source/best-known checkpoint, manifest, parameter, buffer, graph, config, tokenizer and optional runtime/system-state identities; approved objective and primary contrast; intervention/search-space/method/policy identities; pinned engines; permissions; authorized knowledge/teacher inputs; portfolio; baselines; seeds/statistics; runtime; finite budgets; owner approval and promotion/export-parity protocols.

`contract_key` is the declared logical binding key used by a search-space/IR family. It is not the frozen content hash or an authorization credential. This avoids a circular hash where the contract contains a search-space digest whose body contains the contract's own digest. The independent canonical contract digest plus revision must be the authoritative execution/lease binding. Editing any contents yields a different digest even if a caller reuses the logical key. Initial revision has no parent; later revisions require the previous immutable contract identity. T022's coordinator must persist/check that relationship rather than treating a reference as proof.

Gym manifests pin package/name/version/origin, adapter/model interface, code/dependency/image/data identities, input/output schemas, lifecycle/data/scoring/privacy policy records, scorer/oracle, metric domains/units/directions/ranges, trust/isolation policy, validation/approval receipt IDs, usage/confidentiality and finite episode/step/output bounds. These references freeze reset/termination/cleanup, rubric/invalid-output, data provenance and export conditions without importing code into the validator. Their referenced records and actual adapter/sandbox capabilities must be resolved at later intake/preflight. A matching digest proves identity, not scorer truth, license rights or owner approval.

## Portfolio and thresholds

Foundry and user gyms use exactly the same bindings/checks. Gym map keys must equal their complete manifest hashes. Every binding names its exact validation receipt, objective, roles, metric contrasts, schedule and three exact splits. Roles are target, retention, diagnostic, efficiency, safety and independent_verification. Multiple contrasts of one metric are keyed by both metric and comparator. Priority is finite/nonnegative and cannot disable hard checks.

A required metric needs a hard floor, ceiling, max-regression or strictly positive gain. Floors/ceilings respect metric ranges; directions and units remain explicit. Required retention roles must contain protected quality metrics with their own regression limits; safety roles must contain required safety metrics. A primary objective names exactly one required target binding/metric/comparator with positive gain. Capability gain uses task quality; efficiency gain uses actual resource metrics and retains quality constraints without demanding positive accuracy gain. Timing gains require a paired empirical uncertainty policy. Causal claims require a mechanism metric; schema eligibility does not establish a causal result.

The `standard_five_gym` profile requires at least five distinct named required gyms, retention and independent-final coverage, explicit latency/memory ceilings, and complete matched source/no-op/best-known controls. Rebinding a gym or using another version of the same named gym cannot inflate its count. `exploratory` can use a smaller relevant portfolio but remains labeled exploratory; it cannot report passage of standard assurance. Required missing/timed-out/noisy results must remain failed/incomplete/inconclusive in later gates, never vanish into weighted summaries.

## Splits and independent evaluation

Every binding embeds exactly development, selection and holdout manifests with exact dataset/item/group/family, prior exposure ledger and access policy. All required splits are nonempty. Items cannot be repeated within a split, relabeled into different groups/families, or cross canonical roles anywhere in the portfolio. Groups cannot cross roles. Shared items within the same role across gyms remain traceable and require deduplication; they do not increase independent sample counts. New holdouts require known-unexposed history and final feedback closes the revision. Legacy edit/sealed role names are rejected.

A required independent-verification binding and frozen panel/evaluator/administration policy are mandatory. Trusted in-process fixture discipline is limited to fixture-only claims and cannot serve as adversarial or broad generalization isolation. Deterministic exact statistics are likewise fixture-only. A separately declared process/account isolation requirement still needs actual platform qualification; a DTO field does not enforce it. Exposure independence is checked again against the real persistent ledger, not inferred from a split label.

## Runtime, controls and finite work

Controls require original, no-op and best-known arms, exact frozen input models, matched tasks/seeds, no-op apply/export/reload and parameter/inference identity, and positive primary improvement. Optional disabled training baselines may be recorded explicitly; enabled training requires independent permission. Source/no-op/best-known arms cannot be disabled or made optional. No-op timing noise cannot satisfy the strictly positive gain gate by metadata alone.

The runtime pins physical device, driver/backend/dependencies/code/environment, precision, workload lengths and their units, batch/concurrency/cache, synchronization, determinism and state-reset policy. Required latency/memory metrics bind actual measurement scope/statistic/timer/order/warmup/repetitions, matching runtime synchronization and repeat counts. Process RSS, device allocator/device-wide peak and forward/end-to-end latency are distinct scopes, not proxies for one another.

Statistics freeze minimum groups, repeated seeds, confidence/estimator, maximum finalists/comparisons, multiplicity family and stopping rule. Schema checks minimum split inventory, multiplicity for the planned maximum panel, and a conservative mandatory-control/candidate evaluation lower bound. Actual sample completeness and uncertainty decision are later evaluator responsibilities.

Limits explicitly bound wall/CPU/GPU time, attempts/evaluations, programmer/teacher tokens, external spend, host/device memory, storage, workers and retries. Zero external spend/tokens is allowed and disables that work; core execution limits must be positive. Numeric maximum sentinel values are rejected, wall time is capped at seven days, workers at 1024 and per-operation retries at 100. CPU/CUDA synchronization and resource budgets must agree. Teacher/remote payload requests require their own token/permission grants and cannot consume holdout inputs. Required hard-limit enforcement and all actual artifact/authority resolution remain preflight gates; schema success is not an unlimited fallback or scientific status.

Tests exercise valid five-gym and three-gym exploratory metadata, missing/nonfinite/weighted-away gates, role laundering, exact split/group/exposure separation, stale scorer/validation/portfolio IDs, controls/versions, finite budgets, measurement scopes/repeats, optional methods, revisions and efficiency-only primary gains. These are contract validation fixtures, not completed gym experiments.
